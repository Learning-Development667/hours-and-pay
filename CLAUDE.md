# Hours and Pay

A private ledger for Mark and Shelley: hours Mark works at MA Group, with live estimated take-home pay for each pay cycle and every income in one place (MA Group pay, a military pension, PIP). Two people only, each with their own sign-in. Not Daybook, not a Mark 1 Apps product: it never shares code, data or a Firebase project with Daybook.

## Brief (agreed 19 November 2026)
- **Who:** Mark and Shelley. Both can add, change and delete hours, and change settings. Nobody else can sign in.
- **What it does:** log hours by day; work out the hourly rate from the salary and contracted hours; estimate income tax and National Insurance; show a running total for the current pay cycle, the tax year and since tracking began; show the weekly aim against hours worked.
- **Pay cycle:** the 15th to the 14th, paid on the 25th of the month the cycle ends in (hours from 15 Nov to 14 Dec are paid on 25 Dec; MA Group's October 2026 payroll paid hours up to 14 October in October). The military pension is paid on the 22nd; PIP every Tuesday. A pay date on a weekend or an England bank holiday is shown on the working day before (`workingDayOnOrBefore`, `BANK_HOLIDAYS` in `calc.js`, which needs the next year's dates adding each autumn).
- **Look:** premium. Deep navy, gold hairlines, Fraunces for figures and headings, Inter for text. Light and dark.
- **Not:** an App Store app, a payslip, or tax advice. It says so on the page.

## Working rules
- UK English. No em dashes anywhere a person reads.
- **Mark's figures may go in `figures.json` only** (Mark, 21 November 2026: "I am happy to take the risk"; the repository and site are public). It holds just what the sums need: pay, tax codes, year-to-date figures, pension, PIP, sick pay and outgoings. **Never** his National Insurance number, address, date of birth, email addresses, bank details or anything medical, and no hours. Nothing personal anywhere else in the repository; tests use made-up figures. To change his figures, edit `figures.json` and give it a new `stamp` (and a short `note`): the app merges it into `config/main` once, the next time either of them opens it, and shows "Figures updated". Outgoings merge by name, so ones they add in the app are kept.
- `config.js` holds only the public Firebase web settings. The database rules in the Firebase console hold the two real email addresses; `firestore.rules` here is the template with placeholders.
- **Version number:** bump `APP_VERSION` (and `APP_DATE`) in `app.js` and `version` in `version.json` together on every commit that changes the app. The footer and Settings show the version; when `version.json` on the site is newer than the open copy, a banner offers "Update now" (checked on opening, on returning to the app, and every 10 minutes).
- Run `node --check` on every JS file before committing. Bump the `?v=` on `styles.css`, `calc.js`, `app.js` and `config.js` in `index.html` when they change.
- Test with `node tests/app-test.js` (headless Chromium with a stand-in Firebase; checks sign-in, set-up, add, edit, delete, settings, 390 dark and 320 light, no sideways scroll, controls 44px or more). Say plainly what was not tried on an iPhone.
- Anything that stops a save marks the field itself in red with "Missing information" or "Check this".
- Always give Mark the direct link to a console or settings page.

## Shape
- Plain HTML, CSS and JS, no build step. Firebase JS SDK v10 modular from gstatic (Auth email and password, Firestore). Fonts from Google Fonts. The Content Security Policy is in `index.html`.
- `index.html` (sign-in, the ledger, the Change hours, Settings and Set up sheets), `styles.css` (tokens at the top), `calc.js` (dates, pay cycles, tax and NI, the ledger; pure functions), `app.js` (sign-in, rendering, saving), `config.js`, `manifest.json` and the icons.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages from `main`, root, at https://learning-development667.github.io/hours-and-pay/ (the repository is public; see the figures rule above).
- **Firebase project:** Wage Tracker (`wage-tracker-69401`), Spark plan, Firestore in europe-west2 (London). Sign-up is switched off; the two users were added by hand.
- **Data:** `config/main` (salary, contracted hours, weekly aim, tax codes, monthly outgoings, payslip year-to-date figures, pension and the day of the month it is paid (`mpDay`), PIP and its weekday) and `hours/{YYYY-MM-DD}` (one document per day: `date`, `mins`, `holiday`, `shifts` [{`start`,`end`} as HH:MM], `note`, `at`). Older documents with random ids and no shifts are still read and are replaced when that day is saved.
- **Entering hours:** a calendar of the pay cycle, first on the page (beside the pay card on wide screens); tap a day to add up to six shifts (start and finish, same day, no overlaps). Times are typed (0630, 630, 6.30 or 06:30 are tidied to 06:30 by `normTime`) or picked on the clock button's wheel, which opens on the typed time or the shift before, never the clock time. Weekends (Mark does not work them) and past weekdays with no hours are tinted red; sick pay days have their own blue tint and say SSP; holiday is teal; a key sits under the calendar. Hours logged folds away (closed by default; each phone remembers). Who entered the hours is not recorded.
- **Pay:** hours at the hourly rate (salary / 52 / contracted hours, rounded to the penny as payroll does) plus Statutory Sick Pay: a day's rate for each weekday with no hours at all that sits in a run of four or more days in a row without work (weekends count; a day with any hours is not a sick day), from the SSP start date, at most 140 days (28 weeks of 5), counted only for days already passed (`sspDaySet`). The workplace pension is taken after tax (relief at source). Checked against the October 2026 payroll preview: same hours pay, tax and NI.
- **Holiday days:** the day sheet's "Holiday" tick saves `hours/{date}` with `holiday: true` and no shifts. A holiday day pays a full day (`dayRate`: salary / 260, MA Group's own day rate), is never a sick pay day but does not break a run of days off, and future holiday days replace the weekly aim's hours in forecasts. Settings keeps `holidayLeft` (from HR) and `holidayAsOf`; the calendar shows how many are marked and left. Holiday year is January to December.
- **Monthly outgoings** (`config/main.outgoings`, a list of name and monthly amount): listed under the cycle's income with their total and what is left, marked Covered or Short. Edited in their own sheet. A link ending `#outgoings=` and a base64url JSON list of `{name, amount}` opens that sheet filled in (merged by name with the saved list) for checking before Save; the link's data never goes in this repository.
- **Money landing this cycle** (`landingPay`, `runningTotal` in `calc.js`; Mark, 21 November 2026: payroll cut-off is the 14th, so a cycle should tally with the outgoings it has to cover). The income section, the outgoings' Covered or Short line and the running total count money as it lands in the account: the MA Group pay paid on the 25th inside the cycle (for the previous cycle's hours; `config/main.netPaid['YYYY-MM']` holds the payslip's actual take-home once known, otherwise the estimate from that cycle's hours), the pension on its pay day and PIP on its weekday. The running total starts at minus the monthly outgoings and is a step chart (solid so far, dotted to come). The pay card still shows what this cycle's hours will pay on the next 25th.
- **Borrowed money** (`config/main.loan`: `amount`, `months`, `first` 'YYYY-MM' (first repayment's pay month), `from` (date extra hours count from), `paid` {'YYYY-MM': amount}; `loanPlan`, `loanOutIn` in `calc.js`; Mark, 21 November 2026): its own section under the outgoings, a plain summary and a table, no instructions in red. Hours worked above the weekly aim from `from` are earned back off the amount: each cycle's logged hours less the aim for those days, valued at the take-home they add on top of the aim's pay. Holiday never counts ("the loan is paid by extra hours only"): the aim is not lowered for holiday and holiday pay is not counted. Still to earn back shows the hours needed and a week until the end of the last repayment's cycle. Each month in the repayments table is a button that opens the sheet on that month's box, so a bigger payment can be put in ahead of the 25th (shown as Due until its pay day, then Paid). Repayments are money leaving with the MA Group pay on the 25th: each month's (the paid amount once entered, otherwise the share of what is unpaid) is an outgoing, and comes off the running total, in the cycle that pay lands in. `figures.json` may carry `loan`; payments entered in the app are kept.
- **Update links:** a link ending `#update=` and a base64url JSON object (any of the Settings keys, plus `outgoings`) opens "Update your settings", listing each change with its old value; Apply writes them to `config/main`. This is how Claude passes payslip and other personal figures to Mark without putting them in this repository.
- **This tax year** (`yearForecast` in `calc.js`): MA Group pay and tax to date from the payslip figures in Settings, then tracked cycles, then the weekly aim to April; the pension's year from its gross, with tax so far from Settings (`mpTaxYtd`) and the current code for the months left. Shown as one bar against the £12,570 and £50,270 lines, with the likely refund or amount owed after April. Ignores benefits and expenses in the code.
- **Tax:** 2026 to 2027 rates for England. Codes ending Week1/Month1, W1/M1 or X are worked month by month; other codes use the year so far when the payslip's year-to-date figures are filled in, otherwise month by month. BR, D0, D1, NT, 0T and K codes are read.
- **First sign-in:** an empty database opens the Set up sheet, which takes a set-up code (base64 JSON of the settings and any hours) given to Mark privately, or Settings can be filled in by hand.
